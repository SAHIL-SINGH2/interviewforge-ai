# 3–4 minute demo script

0:00 — Open InterviewForge AI and explain: “I provide the exact JD and resume; the system analyzes both before the interview.”

0:20 — Paste/upload the demo JD + resume. Click Analyze. Show role, required skills, behavioral competencies, candidate strengths, missing skills and Job Fit.

0:55 — Click Start personalized interview. Let the first question be spoken. Answer by voice.

1:30 — Show that the next question changes based on the answer. Mention that the state keeps previous answers and moves from Screening to Competency to Deep-Dive.

2:15 — In Deep-Dive, give a short answer containing a vague claim; show the follow-up asking for a metric/technical detail.

2:45 — Finish the interview. Show Overall Score, competency scores, question-level feedback, strengths, weaknesses, preparation gaps and readiness.

3:20 — Open History and show the completed interview stored in SQLite. Briefly toggle the optional camera preview and state that camera is not used for emotion scoring.
